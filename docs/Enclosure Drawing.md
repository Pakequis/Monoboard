# Monoboard Enclosure — Cut List for the Four Pieces

Dimensions for the already-cut enclosure pieces. Thickness is 13 mm on all
four. Dimensions in millimetres, drawings to scale within each diagram.
Assembly confirmed against a build photo: a frame around the e-paper panel,
A as top/bottom and B as sides. The corners butt directly against each other
(no joinery between the wood pieces); the panel is held by channels carved
into the inner face, see the assembly note in `docs/Enclosure Build.md`.

## Notes

**Stock needed:** 2×163 + 2×125 = 576 mm of linear pine stock at 44 mm
width (not accounting for saw kerf between cuts).

**Joinery:** channels carved into the inner face of the pieces hold the
e-ink panel's edge. The top rail and the two side rails form a fixed U
shape (glued and nailed); the bottom rail is loose — the panel slides
through the channels and the bottom rail closes last, with no glue or
nails, so it can be opened again.

**Open:** finish only, see `docs/Enclosure Build.md`.
